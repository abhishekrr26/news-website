21 Killed in Hotel Fire at Delhi's Malviya Nagar, Foreigners Among Victims

In a tragic incident, a fire broke out at a hotel in Delhi's Malviya Nagar, resulting in the death of 21 people, including foreigners. The fire is believed to have started due to an electrical short circuit. Rescue operations are underway, and authorities are investigating the cause of the fire.

India's GDP Growth Slows to 5.5% in Q1 2024

India's GDP growth has slowed to 5.5% in the first quarter of 2024, according to the latest economic data. The slowdown is attributed to a combination of factors, including global economic uncertainties and domestic challenges. Economists are closely monitoring the situation and are hopeful for a rebound in the coming quarters.

New COVID-19 Variant Detected in India

Health authorities in India have detected a new variant of the COVID-19 virus. The variant, which has been named "Delta Plus," is believed to be more transmissible than previous strains. Scientists are conducting further research to understand the implications of this new variant and its potential impact on public health.
