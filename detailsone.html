21 Killed in Hotel Fire at Delhi's Malviya Nagar, Foreigners Among Victims

In a tragic incident, a fire broke out at a hotel in Delhi's Malviya Nagar, resulting in the death of 21 people, including foreigners. The fire is believed to have started due to an electrical short circuit. Rescue operations are underway, and authorities are investigating the cause of the fire.Eight victims remain in critical condition on ventilators at Max Healthcare, suffering primarily from smoke inhalation (asphyxiation) and minor burns. Several patients suffered fractures after jumping from upper floors to escape the blaze, with one patient requiring neurosurgery for a spinal injury. The Delhi Fire Service deployed seven fire tenders initially, later increasing manpower.
The hotel was licensed for only 6 rooms but was allegedly operating 25 rooms under the Delhi government's Bed & Breakfast policy.
The exact cause remains undetermined, though officials suspect it originated from the restaurant. Prime Minister Narendra Modi announced ₹2 lakh compensation for victims' families. Follow-ups include a magisterial inquiry, with the Delhi Police's Crime Branch investigating. The hotel's owner and manager were arrested, and the Delhi government has ordered a safety audit of all hotels in the city. The incident has sparked discussions on fire safety regulations and enforcement in Delhi's hospitality industry.
