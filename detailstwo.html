India's GDP Growth Slows to 5.5% in Q1 2024

India's GDP growth has slowed to 5.5% in the first quarter of 2024, according to the latest economic data. The slowdown is attributed to a combination of factors, including global economic uncertainties and domestic challenges. Economists are closely monitoring the situation and are hopeful for a rebound in the coming quarters.

The economic slowdown has directly affected job creation. Youth unemployment rose to 7.8% in Q1 2024, the highest in nearly two years. Informal sector workers, who constitute over 80% of India's workforce, faced reduced wages and job insecurity. The gig economy, which had absorbed many displaced workers, saw a 12% decline in new onboarding rates. Experts warn that without sustained GDP growth above 7%, India's demographic dividend could turn into a demographic burden

In response to the slowdown, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has maintained an accommodative monetary policy stance, keeping interest rates unchanged at 6.5%. The government has also announced a stimulus package worth ₹1.5 lakh crore aimed at boosting infrastructure development and supporting small and medium enterprises (SMEs). However, analysts caution that these measures may take time to yield results, and the global economic environment remains a significant headwind.

Looking ahead, economists are optimistic that India's GDP growth will rebound in the second quarter of 2024, driven by a recovery in consumer demand and increased government spending. However, they emphasize the need for structural reforms to address underlying issues such as labor market rigidity and infrastructure bottlenecks to sustain long-term growth.
